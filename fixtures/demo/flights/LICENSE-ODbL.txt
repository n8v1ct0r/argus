The flight data in this directory contains information from ADSB.lol (https://adsb.lol),
which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

License text: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Argus redacted these records before storing them:
- Each aircraft address is replaced by a keyed digest. The key is never stored.
- Callsigns and registrations are not stored.
- Aircraft flagged LADD or PIA are left out.

You may use, share, and adapt this data under the ODbL. If you publicly use an adapted
version, offer that version under the ODbL too.
